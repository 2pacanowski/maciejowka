— Willa Maciejówka —

A larch-log house,
standing since 1901.

Built in 1901 and hand-notched by Góral craftsmen, Willa Maciejówka has been carefully restored and fitted with every modern comfort while preserving its historic character.

The ground floor opens to a spacious hall, two bedrooms with double beds, a full bathroom, and a fully-equipped kitchen that flows into a sunny conservatory — the heart of every family gathering.

Upstairs, a cosy living room and two more bedrooms continue the villa's folk-art character, both featuring traditional triangular windows that flood the rooms with mountain light.

Four bedrooms · nine guests · private garden · dogs welcome.

— What's Nearby —

Five minutes,
everywhere worth going.

Restaurants, ski lifts, and the promenade — all walkable from the front gate.

  • Krupówki Promenade The main pedestrian street — shops, oscypek, terraces 5 min walk
  • Gubałówka Funicular Three minutes to the ridgeline — panoramic Tatra views 10 min walk
  • Zakopane Train Station PKP connections to Kraków and Warsaw 15 min walk
  • Termy Zakopane Geothermal pools, outdoor in all weathers 8 min drive
  • Polana Szymoszkowa Family-friendly ski resort, equipment rental on-site 10 min drive
  • Kasprowy Wierch Gondola To the top of Mt. Kasprowy Wierch — 1,987 m 15 min drive
