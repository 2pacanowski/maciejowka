The Villa

Where Mountain Tradition
Meets Modern Comfort

Built in 1901 and listed on Poland's National Register of Monuments, Willa Maciejówka stands on ul. Sienkiewicza — one of Zakopane's most sought-after central streets. The original larch-log structure, hand-notched by local Góral craftsmen, has been carefully restored and fitted with every modern comfort while preserving its historic character.

The ground floor opens into a double-height living room with traditional folk-art details. A fully-equipped large kitchen leads into a sunny conservatory with a generous dining table — the heart of every family gathering. Outside, a private garden offers a quiet retreat just moments from Zakopane's vibrant centre.

Upstairs, four bedrooms — each with large double beds, quality linens and thoughtful folk-art details — sleep up to nine guests in comfort. Three full bathrooms ensure nobody has to queue. Ideal for families with children and groups of friends — dogs are warmly welcome.
