— Why Maciejówka —

Three reasons
guests return.

A listed monument on a prime central street and the quiet hospitality of a hundred-year-old villa.

— I —

Listed
Monument

Traditional pine-log villa in the grand Zakopane style. One of the region's finest examples of late 19th-century architecture registered on Poland's National Register of Monuments. Ornate arched windows, fan-carved woodwork, restored with care.

— II —

Prime
Central
Location

A four-minute walk to Krupówki promenade and Rówień Krupowa park, yet ul. Sienkiewicza stays peacefully still after dark. The promenade by day, the mountain hush by night.

— III —

A House
for Nine

Four bedrooms, a sun-drenched conservatory with dining area, full kitchen and a private garden. Ideal for families travelling together, or one slow-moving group. Dogs welcome.

— Willa Maciejówka —

A pine-log house,
standing since 1901.

Built in 1901 and hand-notched by Góral craftsmen, Willa Maciejówka has been carefully restored and fitted with every modern comfort while preserving its historic character.

The ground floor opens to a spacious hall, two bedrooms with double beds, a full bathroom, and a fully-equipped kitchen that flows into a sunny conservatory — the heart of every family gathering.

Upstairs, a cosy living room and two more bedrooms continue the villa's folk-art character, both featuring traditional triangular windows that flood the rooms with mountain light.

Four bedrooms · nine guests · private garden · dogs welcome.
