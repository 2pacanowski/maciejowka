Conclusion of the Agreement

The subject matter of the Agreement is the rental of an accommodation unit offered by the Service Provider via the Platform. The agreement is concluded through the Electronic Booking Form in successive steps: the Guest specifies the exact check-in and check-out dates, selects the accommodation unit, and declares the number of occupants.

Once the Guest has provided all required information, a booking summary will be displayed. To complete the booking, the Guest must provide the mandatory personal data in the Electronic Booking Form, accept the content of these Terms and Conditions, and submit the booking by clicking the [Place Order] button.

The accommodation rental agreement is deemed concluded at the moment the Service Provider accepts the Electronic Booking Form, as confirmed by the display of a booking confirmation message and the assignment of a booking reference number.

Upon conclusion of the agreement, the Guest will receive an e-mail confirming the agreement and setting out all of its material terms, in particular the Total Service Price, the payment deadline and method, and the booking acceptance and cancellation conditions. Where a prepayment is required, the deadline for making such payment is specified together with information on the consequences of failure to pay by that deadline. Failure to make the prepayment by the deadline specified in the e-mail will result in the automatic cancellation of the booking and withdrawal by the Service Provider from the concluded accommodation rental agreement without the need to set an additional deadline for performance.

Subletting the accommodation unit that is the subject of the Offer, as well as transferring or granting access to it to third parties, is strictly prohibited.

Cancellation and Modification of Bookings

Failure to complete the actions described in the booking confirmation e-mail within the required timeframe will result in the automatic cancellation of the booking and withdrawal by the Service Provider from the concluded Agreement without the need to set an additional deadline for performance.

Cancellation or modification of a booking may be carried out via the link contained in the confirmation e-mail or by contacting Guest Services. Using the link in the e-mail enables automatic and immediate cancellation of the booking on the terms accepted during the booking process through the Electronic Booking Form.

Complaints

In the event of a service being performed in a manner inconsistent with the Agreement, the Guest should submit any complaint in writing or by e-mail within 14 days of the end of the stay.

The complaint should include the Guest's details: first name, surname, the e-mail address provided at the time of booking, and a description of the issue.

The Service Provider will process the complaint within 14 days of receipt and will inform the Guest of the outcome in the same form — written or electronic.

If the information provided in the complaint requires supplementation, the Service Provider will request such supplementation within the timeframe designated for processing the complaint. The period referred to in point 3 runs for the Service Provider from the moment the supplemented complaint is received.

In the event of a complaint being rejected, the Service Provider is obliged to provide a detailed written or electronic statement of the reasons for the rejection.
